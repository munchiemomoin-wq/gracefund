Welcome to
Fundraising Cup
Fundraisingcup.org Ministry is a Non profit donation-based funding organization aimed to supporting women and children in need. The mission of Fundraisingcup.org is to empower local community programs to support the health and quality of life of women and children so that no one is left hungry or isolated. We are excited to welcome our new Marketing, Communications Coordinator, who will help us accomplish our goals of raising funds for local women shelters and children in need of supplies. We created our platform to help the people who require assistance globally.
