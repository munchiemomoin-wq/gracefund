What Is Crowdfunding? 
Crowdfunding Definition
Crowdfunding is the practice of raising funds online with the help of a large group of people who support one’s chosen cause. 
Crowdfunding Meaning
A donation-based crowdfunding website is a platform where you can fundraise for a specific cause and receive money online from people across the country. You can crowdfund for almost anything, whether you want to start a fundraiser for a cause close to your heart or if you want to publish a book or create your own business and need help covering your expenses.
 An individual or NGO that starts the fundraiser is called the campaigner, and people who support the cause by contributing some funds are donors. Anyone in need of financial assistance can create a fundraiser for different causes like medical, personal, social, and creative. People who believe in your cause will contribute to your fundraising campaign. Best crowdfunding sites for personal or social causes make it easier for people to support individuals and communities requiring immediate financial assistance.

How Is Crowdfunding Different From Traditional Fundraising?
To put it simply, consider crowdfunding to be a group assignment.
When a group of people works together to achieve a common goal (in this case, raising funds), each member plays a unique role. Previously, traditional crowdfunding methods such as galas, events, auctions, and rallies were used. Individuals and organizations participate in these events, and the funds raised or donated by participants are used to further the cause of the crowdfunding campaign. The digital world changed the face of crowdfunding over time. It evolved from traditional fundraising methods to online crowdfunding. 
Sometimes, people confuse whether crowdfunding and fundraising are the same or different. Crowdfunding is one part of fundraising. In fundraising, anyone can raise funds from various sources like a corporate, a bank, or a venture capitalist if you are a startup. However, online crowdfunding only comes from people who resonate with your cause. 
In the same way, crowdfunding and traditional fundraising are different altogether. As crowdfunding is being carried out online, it is easy for people from across the country to notice your cause’s importance. People can donate and help you achieve your fundraiser goal quickly with just a click. But compared to online fundraising, traditional fundraising has boundaries, like people apart from the local community may find it challenging to recognize and donate to your cause. 
Traditional fundraising is time-consuming and expensive in relation to online crowdfunding. As in traditional fundraising campaigns, people find meeting people directly to explain their cause time-consuming and challenging. Besides this, they also have to bear the cost of marketing and other variable expenses to promote their campaign to many people.

A great place to start is by asking your network of friends, family, neighbors, and colleagues to share your fundraiser on their social media accounts. What Are The Different Types Of Online Crowdfunding?
ImpactGuru practices donation-based crowdfunding on its platform, i.e., helping people raise money online with no fees involved through donations. Donation-based crowdfunding is legal in India as per SEBI regulations. The different types of online fundraising options include: 
 Medical crowdfunding
Medical fundraising is a process through which individuals, NGOs, communities - basically anyone - can start a medical fundraiser for patients with heart, kidney, rare diseases, cancer - any ailment and diagnosis. Medical crowdfunding also helps frontline workers, acid attack survivors, domestic abuse victims, disaster survivors, and more.
Personal crowdfunding
It can be difficult to see your loved one go through some challenging phase in their life, but you can’t solve their problem of getting their job back or making them healthy again. One thing you can do to manage their challenging time or help reduce their burden is to start a fundraiser on the best crowdfunding sites for a personal cause.
 It is a type of crowdfunding that supports people in covering unanticipated personal costs, like raising funds for a child’s education, helping a family raise funds for their basic necessities, paying monthly rent, helping a friend in need after an accident, loss of a job or any such crisis. 
 You can also start an animal fundraiser to support stray animals’ daily expenses, like providing proper hygiene, healthy food, and regular veterinary care. 
Creative crowdfunding
Suppose a person is working on a creative project or an idea. In that case, they can raise money through creative crowdfunding—for example, A filmmaker planning to make a feature film or starting an innovative research project.
NGO crowdfunding
Any NGO or charitable institution can raise funds for various causes like women’s safety, education, malnutrition, child safety, or any cause they believe in.
Crowdfunding For Education
Anyone who wishes to raise funds for education can opt for crowdfunding. Education crowdfunding is the medium of raising funds from many people online to cover education-related costs like tuition fees, university fees, school infrastructure, teachers’ salaries, etc.
 Education-based fundraising is a trusted and most viable option for students in India who want to pursue studies abroad but are forced to delay or quit due to a lack of financial support.
